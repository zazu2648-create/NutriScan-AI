## Full Description
**NutriScan AI — Scan. Know. Eat Smart.**

Understand what is inside your packaged foods and daily meals in seconds. NutriScan AI combines real-time barcode scanning, verified public food databases, nutrition label OCR, and visual meal photo estimation into a calm, accessible experience built in English and Urdu (RTL).

### Key Features
- **Smart Barcode Scanner**: Scan EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E, and QR retail food barcodes to look up packaged products from Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central.
- **Complete Nutrient Breakdown**: View calories, protein, carbohydrates, total sugars, added sugars, total fat, saturated fat, trans fat, dietary fiber, sodium, and salt—always distinguishing verified database values from OCR extractions and AI estimates.
- **Nutrition Label OCR Scanner**: Photograph a Nutrition Facts table when a product is not yet in the database, review every extracted row, and save it cleanly without converting missing values into zero.
- **AI Meal Photo Estimator**: Snap or upload a plate photo to identify multiple food components, adjust gram portions, and log estimated macros.
- **Smart Serving Calculator**: Switch instantly between 1 serving, 1 packet, half packet, 100g, 50g, or custom gram amounts.
- **Configurable Nutrition Indicators**: Visual alerts for high sugar, high sodium, and high saturated fat using transparent, customizable per-100g reference thresholds.
- **Comparable Alternatives**: Discover comparable category options with lower sugar, lower sodium, higher protein, or higher fiber when complete nutrition data is available.
- **Daily Calorie & Macro Tracker**: Track Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Snacks with a 7-day trend chart, personalized Mifflin-St Jeor target estimates, offline caching, optional cloud sync, and JSON/CSV data export.
- **Bilingual English & Urdu (RTL)**: Switch seamlessly between English and right-to-left Urdu across every screen, plus Light and Dark themes.

### Transparency & Non-Medical Notice
NutriScan AI is an informational nutrition tool and does not provide medical diagnoses or clinical prescriptions. Always verify allergen and ingredient labels directly on physical packaging.
