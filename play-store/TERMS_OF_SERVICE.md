# Terms of Service — NutriScan AI

Effective Date: November 2026  
Application: NutriScan AI (`com.nutriscanai.app`)

## 1. Informational & Non-Medical Purpose
NutriScan AI is an informational nutrition tracking tool. Daily calorie and macronutrient targets (including Mifflin-St Jeor energy estimates) and high-sugar, high-sodium, or high-saturated-fat indicators are general reference tools—**not medical advice, clinical diagnosis, or dietary prescriptions**.

## 2. Data Sources & Accuracy
- **Verified Database Entries**: Sourced from Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central. While we keep records updated and cached responsibly, manufacturers may reformulate products without notice.
- **Label OCR & User Entries**: Extracted text from nutrition labels and manual entries depend on image clarity and user verification.
- **AI Photo Estimates**: Visual meal recognition provides rough estimates only and is never presented as laboratory-verified fact.
- **Allergens**: Always check the physical package label for allergen and ingredient warnings before consuming any food product.

## 3. Acceptable Use & Account Deletion
You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential. You may delete your account and associated cloud data at any time from inside the application.
